# Evelasting V5.2

- All Releases is now a compact, independently vertically scrollable library.
- Six release covers are visible at once on large desktop screens; responsive grids are used on smaller screens.
- Added vertical scroll controls, edge fades, and a themed scrollbar.
- The expanded floating player can be dragged around the viewport and remembers its position.
- The player still first appears in its original position.
- Collapsing the player now leaves only the current artwork icon.
- Hovering or focusing the icon reveals the compact now-playing preview.
- Clicking the collapsed artwork icon restores the full player.
