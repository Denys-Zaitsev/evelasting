# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in the Evelasting project is expected to communicate respectfully, constructively, and professionally.

Examples of positive participation include:

- Giving specific and useful feedback
- Respecting different backgrounds, perspectives, and experience levels
- Focusing discussion on the project and the work
- Accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces used to represent the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and communications that do not follow this standard. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Concerns can be reported privately through the official contact options at [evelasting.com](https://evelasting.com). Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible.
