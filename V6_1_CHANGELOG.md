# Evelasting V6.1

- The collapsed artwork now stays at the full player's previous visual edge instead of jumping toward the centre.
- The collapsed artwork can be dragged anywhere inside the viewport.
- Dragging and clicking are separated: a drag only moves the icon, while a normal click expands the player.
- The icon snaps softly to nearby screen edges and cannot be moved outside the viewport.
- The saved player position continues to persist through localStorage.
- Expanding anchors the full player to the icon while keeping the card inside the viewport.
